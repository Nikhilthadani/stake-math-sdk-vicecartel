# Vice Cartel — Game Overview

**A high-volatility slot game with full-reel Wilds, summed multipliers, four bonus tiers, and premium buy features.**

## How a Normal Spin Works

1. Player places a bet and presses **Spin**
2. Five reels spin and stop, showing a 5×4 grid of symbols
3. Any reel has a small chance (4.5%) to transform into a **full Wild column** with a random **multiplier** (1× up to 1,000×)
4. The game checks all 14 winning lines left-to-right. Wilds substitute for any paying symbol
5. If there are Wild reels, all their multipliers are **added together** and applied to the total line win
6. Scatter symbols (3 or more anywhere) award a small instant payout (0.5×, 2× or 5×). Scatters do **not** start the bonus
7. A hidden **mystery check** can fire after the spin (~1 in 961 spins). Most of the time it starts a **bonus round** (any bonus ~1 in 1,455 spins)

**Example:** Two Wild reels land with 5× and 10× multipliers. The base line wins total 2×. Final payout = 2× × (5+10) = **30× the bet**.

| Feature | What triggers it |
|---------|------------------|
| **Line win** | 3, 4 or 5 matching symbols from the leftmost reel on one of the 14 lines (Wilds substitute) |
| **Full-Reel Wild** | Random 4.5% roll per reel, every spin. Not a symbol landing |
| **Wild multiplier** | Comes with every Wild reel. All Wild multipliers on screen are added together |
| **Scatter pay** | 3 or more SC symbols anywhere on screen. Pays only, never starts a bonus |
| **Mystery check** | Hidden random check after a base or Extra Chance spin (~1 in 961). No symbol is involved |
| **Bonus round** | The mystery check fires and picks a tier (~1 in 1,455 spins) |

| **Extra free spins** | YELLOW on the Bonus Wheel |
| **Golden Case** | Player buys it for 1,000× |
| **Extra Chance** | Player buys it for 3× or 5× per spin |
| **Min 2 Wild Spin** | Player buys it for 200× or 250× |
| **Max win (25,000×)** | Total win reaches the cap, or all 3 letters land in Golden Case. The round ends immediately |

## The Four Bonus Tiers

**What triggers it:** the hidden mystery check after a base or Extra Chance spin. No symbol, scatter or buy triggers a bonus.

When the mystery check fires, one of four bonus levels is selected (or nothing, about a third of the time). Higher tiers are rarer but start much stronger. All tiers are fixed free spins.

| Tier | How often | Free spins | Starts with |
|------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Normal Bonus** | ~1 in 2,401 spins | 10 | No Wilds |
| **Super Bonus** | ~1 in 4,802 spins | 10 | 1 Wild reel at 2× |
| **Hidden Bonus** | ~1 in 19,206 spins | 12 | 2 Wild reels at 3× each (6× from spin 1) |
| **Super Hidden Bonus** | ~1 in 96,032 spins | 12 | 3 Wild reels at 5× each (15× from spin 1) |

- Wilds in the bonus are **sticky** — they stay locked for the whole bonus
- Before each bonus spin, the **Bonus Wheel** may appear (35% chance)
- **Average bonus win: ~830× the bet** (all tiers combined)

## The Bonus Wheel

**What triggers it:** a random 35% roll before each bonus spin. It never appears in the base game.

Three possible outcomes:

| Segment | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **RED** | Adds a new sticky Wild reel with its own multiplier. Occasionally adds 2 at once. |
| **BLUE** | Upgrades an existing Wild's multiplier (+2× up to +1,000×) |
| **YELLOW** | Awards 1 to 3 extra free spins |

The Wheel is the core escalation mechanic — it builds the Wild collection and multipliers throughout the bonus, creating the potential for massive wins.

## Premium Buy Features

Players can purchase direct access to specific game modes:

### Golden Case — 1,000× bet
- **What triggers it:** the player buys it. The max win needs all 3 letters to land
- A single all-or-nothing reveal of three letters: **M, A, X**
- At least one letter always lands
- If all 3 letters land → **instant 25,000× maximum win** (~1 in 26 buys)
- Otherwise → nothing. Pure high-risk, high-reward

### Extra Chance — 3× or 5× bet
- **What triggers it:** the player buys it, per spin. The bonus inside it is still started by the mystery check
- Plays exactly like the normal game, but the **mystery check fires more often**
- At 3× cost: bonus ~1 in 333 spins
- At 5× cost: bonus ~1 in 188 spins
- All payouts are based on the standard 1× bet, not the elevated price

### Minimum 2 Wild Spin — 200× or 250× bet
- **What triggers it:** the player buys it. The 2 Wild reels are forced, not rolled
- A single premium spin **guaranteed to have at least 2 Wild reels**
- Each other reel has a big chance to also become Wild (39.8% at 200×, 44.1% at 250×)
- No bonus can trigger from this spin
- Creates a "power spin" experience with guaranteed multiplier action

## What Makes This Game Unique

1. **Full-Reel Wilds with Summed Multipliers** — every Wild reel adds its own multiplier. Two Wilds at 10× and 25× = 35× every line win.

2. **Four-Tier Bonus Ladder** — from the accessible Normal Bonus to the legendary Super Hidden, each tier starts stronger and offers higher win potential.

3. **Golden Case Buy** — a ~1-in-26 shot at the maximum win creates a unique adrenaline moment distinct from the main game.

4. **Escalating Wheel Mechanic** — the longer a bonus lasts, the more Wilds accumulate and the higher multipliers climb.
