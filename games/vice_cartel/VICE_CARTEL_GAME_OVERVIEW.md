## How a Normal Spin Works

1. Player places a bet and presses **Spin**
2. Five reels spin and stop, showing a 5×4 grid of symbols
3. Any reel has a small chance (~4.5%) to transform into a **full Wild column** — the entire reel glows and shows a random **multiplier** (1× up to 1,000×)
4. The game checks all 14 winning lines left-to-right. Wilds substitute for any paying symbol
5. If there are Wild reels, all their multipliers are **added together** and applied to the total win
6. Scatter symbols (3 or more anywhere) award a small instant payout
7. A hidden check runs every spin (~1-in-984 chance) — if it fires, a **bonus round begins**

**Example:** Two Wild reels land with 5× and 10× multipliers. The base line wins total 2×. Final payout = 2× × (5+10) = **30× the bet**.

## The Four Bonus Tiers

When the hidden mystery check triggers, one of four bonus levels is selected. Higher tiers are rarer but dramatically more rewarding.

### Normal Bonus (most common — ~1 in 2,460 spins)
- **10 free spins** with no starting Wilds
- Each spin, a **Bonus Wheel** may appear (35% chance)
- The Wheel can add Wild reels, upgrade multipliers, or give extra spins
- Wilds earned are **sticky** — they stay locked for the entire bonus
- **Average win: 173× the bet**

### Super Bonus (~1 in 4,920 spins)
- **10 free spins**, but starts with **1 Wild reel already at 2×**
- Same Wheel mechanics — builds on top of the starting Wild
- **Average win: 613× the bet**

### Hidden Bonus (~1 in 19,679 spins)
- Starts with **2 Wild reels at 3× each** (6× multiplier from spin 1!)
- Player **chooses** between:
  - **Spins Mode:** 12 guaranteed free spins
  - **Survival Mode:** 5 lives — each spin risks losing a life, but can last much longer
- **Average win: 4,664× the bet**

### Super Hidden Bonus (extremely rare — ~1 in 98,395 spins)
- Starts with **3 Wild reels at 5× each** (15× multiplier from spin 1!)
- Same choice: Spins (12) or Survival (5 lives)
- Over half of Survival Mode rounds in this tier hit the **25,000× maximum win**
- **Average win: 12,050× the bet**

## The Bonus Wheel

Appears during bonus rounds (35% chance per spin). Three possible outcomes:

| Segment | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **RED** | Adds a new sticky Wild reel with its own multiplier. Occasionally adds 2 at once. |
| **BLUE** | Upgrades an existing Wild's multiplier (can add up to +1,000×) |
| **YELLOW** | Awards extra free spins (or extra lives in Survival Mode) |

The Wheel is the core escalation mechanic — it builds the Wild collection and multipliers throughout the bonus, creating the potential for massive wins.

---

## Survival Mode

Available when the player triggers Hidden or Super Hidden Bonus. Instead of a fixed number of spins, the player gets **5 lives**.

- Each spin has a chance of costing a life (a "dead spin")
- The more Wilds on the board, the **safer** each spin becomes
- With 5 Wilds (all reels), the player is effectively immortal
- The Wheel can award **extra lives** (via YELLOW) to extend the run

This creates genuine tension — the player watches their lives deplete while trying to accumulate enough Wilds to survive and multiply their wins.

## Premium Buy Features

Players can purchase direct access to specific game modes:

### Golden Case — 1,000× bet
- A single all-or-nothing spin
- Each reel independently shows a Golden Briefcase symbol (52% chance)
- If all 5 reels show briefcases → **instant 25,000× maximum win**
- Otherwise → nothing. Pure high-risk, high-reward
- Typically 2-3 briefcases land, creating near-miss excitement

### Extra Chance — 3× or 5× bet
- Plays exactly like the normal game, but the **bonus trigger rate is boosted**
- At 3× cost: bonus triggers 4.5× more often (~1-in-220 spins)
- At 5× cost: bonus triggers 7.9× more often (~1-in-124 spins)
- All payouts are based on the standard 1× bet, not the elevated price

### Minimum 2 Wild Spin — 200× or 250× bet
- A single premium spin **guaranteed to have at least 2 Wild reels**
- Remaining reels may also become Wild naturally
- Creates a "power spin" experience with guaranteed multiplier action

## What Makes This Game Unique

1. **Full-Reel Wilds with Summed Multipliers** — not just a single multiplier, but every Wild reel adds its own, and they compound. Two Wilds at 10× and 25× = 35× every line win.

2. **Four-Tier Bonus Ladder** — from accessible Normal Bonus to the legendary Super Hidden, each tier starts stronger and offers exponentially higher win potential.

3. **Player Agency in Survival Mode** — the choice between guaranteed spins and high-risk survival adds a strategic layer uncommon in slots.

4. **Golden Case Buy** — a pure 3.84% coin-flip for the maximum win creates a unique adrenaline moment distinct from the main game.

5. **Escalating Wheel Mechanic** — the longer a bonus lasts, the more Wilds accumulate and the higher multipliers climb, creating natural narrative tension.
