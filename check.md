# Vice Cartel — Game Summary

**Target RTP: 96.00%** | 5 reels x 4 rows | 14 paylines | Max Win: 25,000x

---

## 1. Base Game (40.33% of RTP)

- Player spins a 5x4 grid with 8 symbols (4 High, 4 Low).
- Each reel has a **4.5% chance** to turn into a **full Wild reel** (all 4 rows become Wild).
- Wild reels get a random **multiplier** (1x to 1000x, weighted toward low values).
- If multiple reels go Wild, their multipliers are **added together** and applied once to the total win.
- **Scatter** symbols (3+ anywhere) pay a flat bonus (5x to 500x bet).

---

## 2. Bonus Trigger — Mystery System (55.67% of RTP)

Every base spin has a hidden **0.1% chance** (~1 in 959 spins) to trigger a bonus.

When triggered, one of these outcomes is drawn:

| Outcome | Chance | What Happens |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Normal Bonus** | 40% | 10 Free Spins, no starting Wilds |
| **Super Bonus** | 20% | 10 Free Spins, 1 Wild reel at 2x |
| **Hidden Bonus** | 5% | 12 Spins or 5 Lives, 2 Wilds at 3x |
| **Super Hidden Bonus** | 1% | 12 Spins or 5 Lives, 3 Wilds at 5x |
| **No Bonus** (near-miss) | 34% | Nothing happens |

---

## 3. Inside the Bonus — The Wheel

On every bonus spin, a **Bonus Wheel** has a **35% chance** to spin.

| Segment | Chance | Effect |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **RED** | 35% | Adds a new sticky Wild reel (10% chance for 2 instead of 1) |
| **BLUE** | 40% | Upgrades a random Wild's multiplier (+2x to +1000x) |
| **YELLOW** | 25% | Adds +1, +2, or +3 extra spins (or lives in Survival) |

Wild reels added by RED **stay for the rest of the bonus**.

---

## 4. Bonus Modes

**Spins Mode** (Normal & Super always; Hidden & Super Hidden by choice):
- Fixed number of spins. YELLOW adds more spins. Game ends when spins run out.

**Survival Mode** (Hidden & Super Hidden by choice):
- Start with 5 lives instead of a spin counter.
- Each spin has a chance to lose 1 life (based on how many Wilds you have):

| Wild Reels | Chance to Lose a Life |
|-----------|----------------------|
| 0 | 92% |
| 1 | 83% |
| 2 | 70% |
| 3 | 52% |
| 4 | 30% |
| 5 | 0% (immortal!) |

More Wilds = safer. At 5 Wilds, you can never lose a life. Lives cap at 5.

## 6. Golden Case (Buy Feature)

- Costs **1,000x** bet to buy.
- Three letters **M**, **A**, **X** can land on the grid.
- If all 3 appear = instant **25,000x MAX WIN**.
- Chance of winning: **3.84%** | RTP: **96.00%**
- Cannot be triggered from normal play — buy only.

## 8. Win Cap

All wins in a single round (base + bonus combined) are **capped at 25,000x total bet**.
If the cap is hit during a bonus, the round ends immediately.
